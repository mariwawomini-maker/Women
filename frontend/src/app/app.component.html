ABOUT THE MUSEUM

SHE STANDS

A living institution dedicated to preserving, honoring and sharing the stories of South Sudanese women.

OUR PURPOSE

To document sacrifice, resilience, leadership, peacebuilding, cultural continuity and nation-building.

RECOGNIZING HER PAST. CELEBRATING HER PRESENT. BUILDING HER FUTURE.
OUR PROMISE

Her story belongs
in the national story.

The museum is a place of remembrance and discovery - connecting elders, young people, researchers, artists, educators and communities.
