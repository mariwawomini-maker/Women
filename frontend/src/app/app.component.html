01 / ABOUT THE MUSEUM

History has more than one voice.

She Stands Women’s Museum preserves, documents, interprets and shares the histories, memories, achievements and lived experiences of women connected to South Sudan.

The museum brings together objects, photographs, oral histories, cultural heritage, biographies, research and learning experiences. It makes space for women’s contributions to liberation, peacebuilding, family and community life, leadership, creative expression and everyday resilience.

We do not tell one single story of South Sudanese women. We make room for many stories to be encountered, questioned, researched and preserved.

OUR MISSION

Preserve with care.

Document and interpret women’s lived experiences, leadership and sacrifices through collections, oral histories, exhibitions, education and community engagement.

OUR VISION

Make women central.

Build a museum where women’s historical, cultural and civic contributions are recognised as central to South Sudan’s past, present and future.
